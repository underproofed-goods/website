02 / Technical Reference

Quick Rhythm Reference

  1. Top-Right: Move strand straight down to bottom-right slot.
  2. Bottom-Left: Move strand straight up to top-left slot.
  3. Rotate: Turn disk 90° counter-clockwise and repeat sequence.
