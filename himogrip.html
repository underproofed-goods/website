HimoGrip Kumihimo Braiding Disk

This innovative Kumihimo disk allows you to maintain tension with multiple types of cord and won't wear out like a foam disk. It has the durability of a wooden disk, without the downsides: thoughtfully engineered slots are designed to grip the cord to maintain tension, but the cord can be easily removed when it's time to move to the next position.

Has been tested and confirmed to work with yarn, Chinese knotting cord, and embroidery floss.

Create braids and friendship bracelets with ease. Toss it in your bag and work on projects on-the-go. Kids and adults alike will enjoy making braids and bracelets on this disk.

  • —
    16-Slot Flexibility: Features 16 total slots with clear markers in 8 positions, making it easy to use as an 8-slot disk.
  • —
    Indexing Marker: One slot has a special mark if you need to keep track of indexing.
  • —
    Original USA Design: Created and manufactured in the USA by Underproofed — not available anywhere else.
