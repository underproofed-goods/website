Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about compatibility, materials, and ordering.

Which braiding discs fit the HimoGrip? ▼

The HimoGrip's modular locking collar fits standard 6-inch and 4.25-inch dense EVA foam Kumihimo disks (both round and square), as well as custom 3D-printed braiding wheels.

Why is the HimoGrip 3D-printed in PETG instead of PLA? ▼

PETG offers superior flexural strength, impact resistance, and tolerance to hand oils and temperature fluctuations. It won't become brittle or deform under continuous cord tension.

How does it reduce hand strain during long projects? ▼

The anatomical palm contour allows your hand to rest in a neutral grip angle, shifting strain away from finger pinch joints to the palm and forearm.
