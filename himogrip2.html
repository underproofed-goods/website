04 / Frequently Asked Questions
Which braiding discs fit the HimoGrip system?
The modular locking collar fits standard 6-inch and 4.25-inch dense EVA foam Kumihimo disks (both round and square), as well as Underproofed rigid custom braiding wheels.
Why is this tool fabricated in PETG instead of PLA?
PETG offers superior flexural strength, impact tolerance, and resistance to skin oils and moisture. It will not warp or become brittle over time under sustained braid tension.
How does it reduce hand strain during long projects?
The anatomical palm swell allows your hand to rest in a neutral grip angle, shifting strain away from finger pinch joints to the palm and forearm.
