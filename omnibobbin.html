OmniBobbin Multi-Slot Bobbins

Keep your long fiber cords organized and tangle-free, with no loose ends. The OmniBobbin is a compact, self-locking fiber spool designed specifically for Kumihimo braiding, macrame, and multi-strand cord work.

Traditional bobbins are flimsy and struggle to grip varied cord sizes. The OmniBobbin features engineered anchor slots that hold cord ends securely without tying knots, and let you adjust working length in seconds.

Works with yarn, c-lon, s-lon, Chinese knotting cord, microcord, and embroidery floss.

  • —
    Multi-Slot Tension Lock: Multiple retention notches grip varying cord thicknesses firmly to prevent unintended unraveling.
  • —
    Compact & Snag-Free: Smooth polymer profile rolls freely without catching adjacent threads on your braiding loom.
  • —
    Packs of 8 & 16 Available: Sized perfectly to equip standard 8-strand or 16-strand Kumihimo setups.
