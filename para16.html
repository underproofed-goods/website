Engineered for 550 Paracord

Para16 Paracord Kumihimo Disk

Tired of ruining foam disks when working with thick cord? The Para16 is custom-engineered with wider, precision slots designed specifically to grip 550 paracord and heavy craft fibers without slot deformation or loss of tension.

It delivers the rigid, lifetime durability of a solid disk while eliminating the frustration of loose braids. Cord snaps firmly into place to hold your work and pops out smoothly when rotating to the next position.

Perfect for paracord bracelets, heavy lanyards, dog leashes, and keychains. Compact and rugged enough to throw in your backpack for field and travel projects.

  • —
    Optimized 550 Paracord Slots: Wider geometry engineered to grip thick outer sheaths securely without binding.
  • —
    16-Slot / 8-Position Layout: 16 total slots with 4 reference markers for easy 8-strand braiding setups.
  • —
    Indexed Alignment Slot: Includes a dedicated marker slot to track indexing on complex multi-stage patterns.
  • —
    Rigid Technical Polymer: Solid-wall PETG fabrication that will not warp, crush, or degrade over time. Made in Michigan, USA.
